About our café

A neighbourhood coffee shop with fresh drinks, sweet treats and a few furry friends.

A cosy place in Astana

We bring together carefully prepared coffee, fresh pastries and a welcoming place to slow down. Meet a friend for a cappuccino or relax with a book beside a sleepy cat. Our coffee counter is separate from the cats' quiet resting space.

A few friendly rules

  • Let the cats come to you.
  • Do not wake sleeping cats.
  • Do not feed cats human food.
  • Keep your voice calm and gentle.
