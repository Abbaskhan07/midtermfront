About Daily Cup

A small coffee shop idea built around simple daily pleasures.

Our idea

Daily Cup is a fictional coffee shop in Astana. We designed it as a welcoming place for students and anyone who enjoys a quiet coffee break.

Our menu keeps things simple: familiar coffee drinks, fresh pastries and a few sweet treats.

What matters to us

  • Carefully prepared coffee
  • A friendly and comfortable atmosphere
  • A simple menu with clear prices
